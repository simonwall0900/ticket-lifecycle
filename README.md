<p>
The next step in the osTicket workflow is creating a support ticket to document and track a customer's technical issue. Tickets can be used for a variety of IT problems, including computers that will not power on, password resets, software issues, system reboots, and other technical requests.

Ticket Creation Process Identify the customer's technical issue. Create a new support ticket in osTicket. Enter the customer's contact information. Document the issue clearly and accurately. Include any relevant troubleshooting information. Submit the ticket for review and assignment.

If a customer contacts the help desk by phone and has not already submitted a ticket, the IT Help Desk Technician can create the ticket on the customer's behalf. This ensures that the issue is properly documented and can be tracked throughout the support process.

Result

Creating a ticket provides a centralized record of the customer's issue. This allows help desk employees to track communication, document troubleshooting steps, assign the request to the appropriate department or technician, and work toward a timely resolution.

<p>
Effective communication is an important part of providing quality IT support. Keeping customers informed throughout the support process helps build trust and ensures they understand the status of their issue.

Customer Communication

When working on a support ticket, IT technicians should:

Provide timely updates to the customer. Clearly explain the issue and troubleshooting process. Set realistic expectations for resolution times. Communicate even when a solution has not yet been identified. Document important communication within the support ticket.

Consistent communication helps customers understand that their issue is being actively addressed.

Team Communication

Communication between IT team members is equally important. Technicians should share relevant information, discuss potential solutions, and provide updates on assigned tickets.

This allows the IT team to:

Work more efficiently. Avoid duplicated troubleshooting efforts. Share knowledge and possible solutions. Maintain accurate ticket information. Resolve technical issues more effectively. Result

Maintaining clear and consistent communication with both customers and team members improves the overall support process. It helps ensure that tickets are properly managed, customers remain informed, and IT technicians can work together to resolve issues efficiently.

<p>
Once a customer's technical issue has been successfully resolved, the support ticket should be properly documented and closed. Closing the ticket confirms that the requested service has been completed and ensures the support team maintains an accurate record of the work performed.

Ticket Closure Process Confirm that the customer's issue has been resolved. Document the troubleshooting steps and solution in the ticket. Add any relevant notes or information for future reference. Confirm that the customer has received the appropriate assistance. Update the ticket status to Resolved or Closed. Review the ticket to ensure all required information has been documented. Result

Properly documenting and closing a ticket creates an accurate record of the support request and the work performed. This information can be used for future troubleshooting, reporting, performance tracking, and auditing purposes.

This completes the ticket lifecycle from ticket creation to resolution and closure.
